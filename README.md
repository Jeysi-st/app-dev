## My Favorite Series: Avatar: The Last Airbender

**Avatar: The Last Airbender** is an animated series that first aired in *2005*. It follows Aang, the last Airbender, as he learns to master all four elements and end a hundred-year war.

> "Sometimes life is like this dark tunnel." - Uncle Iroh

### Why I Like It

- The **characters** grow and change over the three seasons
- The *world-building* is detailed and creative
- It mixes ~~boring~~ serious themes with a lot of humor
